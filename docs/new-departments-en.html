Two new departments: Series Studio and Equity Research

Between October 7 and 10, 2026, the Mega-X console gained two departments. Series Studio turns a one-line brief into an episodic web series, from story to finished cut. Equity Research produces single-stock research, valuation, and risk reports.

Series Studio turns a one-line brief into an episodic web series. The coordinator only dispatches work. Seven roles hand over files in a fixed order: lock the assets, then storyboard, then video. Each step waits for the user's confirmation before the next one starts. The README's sample brief is “8 episodes, 90 seconds each, vertical 9:16 urban mystery series.”

What the catalog tagline means:

Episode story
A full-series outline, the novel text for each episode, and a script for each episode.
Locked assets
Each character, location, and prop gets a written spec plus a text-free PNG concept image. Storyboards and video may only reference these; they never describe a new look.
Finished cut
One mp4 per episode with burned-in subtitles. The first version ships without a cover.

Team

The coordinator is always on; the other seven roles are spawned as needed. The department has no reviewer or ops role.

Workflow

  • The coordinator dispatches one step at a time, and roles cannot dispatch other roles. Passing the format check (format_ok) is not the same as user confirmation.
  • Asset locking: before episode 1 is storyboarded, every recurring character, location, and prop must have a concept image. Slugs cannot be renamed or invented; differences such as costume or day and night are separate variants.
  • Revisions always ship as a new version. Items downstream along the dependency chain are marked stale, then redone and reconfirmed one by one.

Outputs

Everything lands in board/dept-series/series/<project_id>/, split into five folders (00-management through 04-delivery: management, series, assets, episodes, delivery), with 17 deliverable types in total. Each item is versioned from .v001 upward and never overwritten. An item is in one of five states: draft, confirmed, rejected, superseded, or stale.

Dependencies

  • Generation services: video on seedance-2.5 (4–30 s per clip, with audio), with minimax-hailuo-h3 as the fallback. Images come from gpt-image-2, gemini-3.1-flash-image, nano-banana-pro, and other channels. ffmpeg assembles the cut.
  • Toonflow reference snapshot: 223 technique documents and prompts taken from the open-source Toonflow-app (commit 32ac57d, Apache-2.0). The bible maps onto 13 art-style folders and 14 genre folders. It is reference material only; nothing at runtime depends on Toonflow code or services.
  • Department toolkit dept-series.tools: series_store.py manages the versioned project folder and series_cut.py does the editing. The department registers no MCP server of its own.

Relation to Short Drama and Short Film Studio

Series Studio was registered on 2026-10-09 at 18:07 alongside Short Film Studio. An hour later (ai_native e1fb44a), both older departments were deleted and marked “department retired” in the roster, and every content template was pointed at Series Studio. The repo has no record of files migrated from the old departments. Series Studio's method comes from the Toonflow port, not from its predecessors.

Equity Research produces an HTML research report from a stock code. The platform collects data, computes, and writes the report by fixed rules. The department lead, investment-lead, explains the report's inputs, version, valuation assumptions, and risks, and never runs research itself. The engine is a pinned build of the open-source UZI-Skill (MIT license, version 3.9.4). The platform calls it through a bridge and does not modify its source.

Coverage

MarketCodesDepthsMeasured timeReport contents
China A-shares6-digit code; .SH / .SZ / .BJ detected automaticallyQuick overview, Standard researchQuick: about 1.5–4 minScores on 23 dimensions, rule-based commentary from 9 investment schools, scenario estimates
US stocksNYSE / Nasdaq common stocks, priced in USDQuick overview onlyAbout 30 sPrice, financials, valuation, risk, sources and gaps; no overall score or buy/sell advice
  • The third depth, Deep research, is not available. It needs real model analysis and review before it can be called complete.
  • Time limits per run: 600 s for quick, 1200 s for standard. Hong Kong stocks, ETFs, ADRs, and OTC securities are not supported. Reports come in Chinese and English.

Research flow

  1. In New research, the user picks a market, enters a code, chooses a depth, and submits. The API returns a task ID immediately.
  2. The task runs asynchronously on the company's own Task Runtime in three stages: collect, compute, and write the report. It can be paused, resumed, or cancelled between stages. Each company can queue at most 5 tasks.
  3. A failed or interrupted task can be retried. A retry creates a new run and never overwrites the earlier report.
  4. Delivery is three files: report.html for online preview, standalone.html for offline download, and result.json for structured results. Each file's size and SHA-256 are verified, with 50 MB total at most. Before opening a preview, the frontend checks that the link is on the same origin as the API.

Investor conversations and the investment roundtable

  • Investor conversations: pick one investor and ask freely, or attach a finished report and keep asking. 31 investors can hold a conversation, including Buffett, Munger, Soros, Duan Yongping, and Zhang Kun. The Investment schools catalog lists 9 schools and 66 people; the rest are marked as research review only. The English UI hides two Chinese schools.
  • Investment roundtable: pick 2–4 investors, who each speak once in a fixed order. Later speakers respond only to earlier turns that succeeded. There is no moderator summary.
  • Boundaries: the UI labels this “Simulated investment approaches, not the individuals' views.” Investors have no tools and no live quotes, and may cite only report sections. Limits: 2 concurrent rounds per company, 1 per conversation, 120 s per call.

Using them in the console

Series Studio works like any other built-in department: it comes in with a template or is added from the marketplace. Equity Research must be installed separately by the company owner, and only then does the Research tab appear in the company sidebar.

Status and limits

Both departments run their main flow end to end, but each has gaps. The largest: series projects don't appear on the Output Center board, and Deep research and US standard research are not open yet.
